authoring database. Preserve source hashes, provenance and the spatial package's

public runtime package; the indoor catalog publishes identity metadata only.

not public source. Test identities are explicitly disposable fixtures. Simulated
and replayed measurements remain labeled and do not establish physical
commissioning, measured savings or hardware certification.
